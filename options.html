These extensions gained new permissions after an update. They will be turned off on protected pages again until you re-approve them.

    Protected sites

    On these sites (and their subdomains) all extensions are turned off, except the allowed ones. Protected mode stays active as long as a tab of a protected site is open anywhere.

    Automatic protection

    Exceptions
    Sensitive fields on these sites (and their subdomains) never trigger protection, for example sites with a hidden login form on every page. Sites in your protected list are always protected.

      After leaving

      For each site above you can also turn on Log out when leaving: the site's cookies and site data are then cleared, so extensions can't take over your session.

      Always on

      These extensions stay on on all protected pages (including pages with sensitive fields), for example your password manager. Everything else, including newly installed extensions, is turned off. If an extension here gains new permissions after an update, it is removed from this list automatically.

        Activity log

          Good to know

          • Password fields are detected even when hidden or inside (open) Shadow DOM — you never need to click them.
          • After turning extensions off, the page is reloaded so scripts that extensions had already loaded are gone.
          • With several protected sites open at once, only extensions allowed on all open sites stay on.
          • If you turn an extension on yourself during protected mode, it is turned off again right away. Add it to "Always on" or use "Pause".
          • Extensions that can manage other extensions (like 1Password) may be "Always on", but if they turn another extension on during protected mode, Exterminator turns it off again immediately.
          • Don't remove or disable Exterminator while protected mode is active: extensions it turned off would stay off.
          • Extra layer: set your password manager's site access to "On click" in chrome://extensions.
