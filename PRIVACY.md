# Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Tab Chaperone is a Chrome extension. Sander Tamaela develops and publishes it as a private person. The source code is public at https://github.com/Tamaela/tab-chaperone.

## What Tab Chaperone reads

- The addresses of your open tabs and the pages they load. Tab Chaperone compares them with the sites you protect.
- The list of your installed extensions with their names and permissions. Tab Chaperone turns them off and on and shows their risk.
- With automatic protection turned on, whether a page has a password, code or payment field. The detector reports only that such a field exists and when it is gone. It does not read what you type or what the page shows.

## What Tab Chaperone stores

Tab Chaperone stores its settings, its state and an activity log in your browser with `chrome.storage`. The activity log keeps the last 200 entries, such as the sites that were protected and the extensions that were turned off. You can clear it on the settings page. Removing Tab Chaperone removes all of this data.

## What Tab Chaperone sends

Tab Chaperone sends no data. It makes no network requests and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing is shared with the developer or with third parties.

## What Tab Chaperone deletes

For sites with the level Strict, Tab Chaperone clears the cookies and site data of that site when you leave it, and it clears the clipboard. These actions happen in your browser only.

## Limited Use

Tab Chaperone's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Tab Chaperone uses information only to provide its single purpose: protecting the websites you choose from your other extensions.
